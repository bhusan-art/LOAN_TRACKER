How interest works

Full cycles earn exactly the rate

₹1,00,000 at 2% a month earns ₹2,000 every cycle, whatever the month's length.

Partial cycles are pro-rated by days

A short first period, a mid-cycle payoff or a rate change is charged for the days it covers.

Payments pay interest first

Penalty → oldest interest due → principal. Anything left over is held as credit.
